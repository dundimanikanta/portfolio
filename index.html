CooperDB

Go · Log-structured key-value storage engine

A Bitcask-style storage engine with crash recovery, online compaction and thread-safe concurrent access. Every read is one hash lookup plus one disk read.

Syncing every write to disk costs about 2,500× in throughput, and a failed fsync halts the database, because retrying can silently lose data.
